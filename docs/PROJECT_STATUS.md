# Project status and scope

## Books and language editions

* Boys' guide: 23 chapters; girls' guide: 26 chapters.
* Target: English, Arabic and German editions of each guide.
* Tone: pastoral, clear and age-appropriate for Coptic Orthodox teenagers.

## Research phases

- Phase 0: editorial baseline and decisions completed.
- Phase 1: knowledge-base architecture completed.
- Phase 2: 77 source-discovery records after consolidation; research gaps remain.
- Phase 3: pilot evidence for B04 and G05. Claimed evidence is source-reviewed, **not expert-approved**.
- Phase 4: evidence-first CLI prototype; does not call an LLM API.
- Phase 5 onward: pilot writing, full manuscripts, expert review, design and publication pending.

## Mandatory safeguards

No automatically generated text is publication approved. Ensure theological, medical, safeguarding, language and copyright review. The Coptic pastoral question concerning menstruation and Communion requires qualified clergy review. Publication permissions for translations and quotations are unresolved.
