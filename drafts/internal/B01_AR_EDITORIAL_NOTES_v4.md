# B01 Arabic Reader Draft v4 — Biblical Narrative Note

**Narrative:** Zacchaeus, Luke 19:1–10.  
**Use:** Labels, failure, personal dignity, repentance, and practical repair.  
**Commentary basis:** Coptic Orthodox commentary on Luke 19 explains Zacchaeus’s
desire to see Christ, his humble step, Christ’s personal call, and repentance
expressed in restitution. The reader application is original and modest: the story
does not erase responsibility, but it refuses to reduce a person to a label.

No direct commentary quotation is used. Rights, theological, language, and
publication holds remain open.
