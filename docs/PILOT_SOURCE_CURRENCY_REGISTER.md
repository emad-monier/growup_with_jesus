# Pilot Source Currency and Retrievability Register — Step 3

**Scope:** Sources linked to B04/G05 claims in the canonical Phase 3 database.  
**Access-review date:** 2026-11-07.  
**Purpose:** Record source availability, stated dates, edition limits, and follow-up
requirements. It does not verify every claim, change a claim/evidence status, or
substitute for clinical, theological, safeguarding, or rights review.

## Status key

- **Accessible / dated:** The live page was reached and displays a date.
- **Accessible / undated:** The live page was reached but no edition/publication date
  was established.
- **Tool-restricted:** A live-page review was blocked by the audit client; previous
  project metadata or HTTP access does not prove current text/version.
- **Historical:** The source itself is dated and must not be presented as current
  guidance without qualified confirmation.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. Every reviewed source remains subject to its database rights restriction
   (`citation_and_paraphrase_only` or `link_only`); no direct quotation clearance
   was found.
2. Two NHS pages used for G05 have an elapsed “next review” date. They should be
   treated as candidates for expert verification, not automatically discarded or
   silently accepted.
3. The 2015 ACOG opinion and 2015 AAP page are historically useful but cannot be
   the sole basis for current clinical content.
4. Bot/payment restrictions prevented independent browser retrieval of NHS Inform
   and ACOG pages. This is an access limitation, not a finding that their content is
   unavailable or invalid.
5. The live pastoral pages establish retrievability of hosted text, not its
   publication rights, definitive edition, or universal contemporary practice.

## Step 3 gate decision

**Result: REVIEW PACKETS MAY BE PREPARED; CLINICAL AND PASTORAL CONTENT REMAINS
ON HOLD.**

No source or claim status has changed. Before any internal draft can rely on the
excluded hygiene/product or specific safeguarding leads, a later review record must
identify the reviewed source version, reviewer domain, decision, and scope.
