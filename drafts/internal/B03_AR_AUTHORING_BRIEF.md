## Boundaries held in this draft

- Fictional opening scene; no real minor represented, and no claim that it is a true story.
- Anger, sadness, and loneliness are never called sin, defect, or weak faith.
- No general Church ruling on fasting, Communion, or local practice.
- No derived medical or psychological diagnosis; escalation language leads to a trusted
  adult rather than to self-diagnosis.
- St John Chrysostom, *On Divine Providence*, is mapped to B03 but remains
  `intro_verified` only, so no patristic claim was taken from it.
- The St Poemen saying stays excluded pending ISS-003 verification.
- Bishop Moussa, *مكونات الشخصية الإنسانية*, remains `catalog_verified_pdf_not_examined`,
  so no content claim was taken from it in v1.
- Both Christ-life scenes are told only from the cited biblical text; no dialogue,
  feeling, or motive is invented, and each reference is named in the reader copy.
- Each scene stays in context (before the arrest; at the tomb before the raising) and its
  application uses the modest bridge «قد يضيء لنا هذا الموقف…» plus a named commentary.
- The two church-encyclopedia commentary pages are consulted but not cleared for
  republication, and are not yet registered in the source manifest.
- Reader layer is clean of evidence IDs, review flags, and database language.
- Every substantive change requires a new version plus a synchronized RTL HTML copy.
