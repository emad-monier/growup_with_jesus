## Mandatory holds

1. A clinician must review final wording on age thresholds, symptoms, pain, heavy bleeding, and any product/hygiene detail.
2. A safeguarding reviewer must approve the school, privacy, and trusted-adult wording for each publication context.
3. A qualified Coptic Orthodox reviewer must assess any later expansion concerning menstruation and Communion. This reader draft deliberately gives no universal rule.
4. A native Arabic youth editor must review readability, regional neutrality, and pastoral register.
5. No direct quotation, Bible wording, or source reproduction may be added without rights and wording review.

## Reader-layer integrity

The reader draft intentionally contains no citation tokens, review markers, source-register language, or final references. Those remain in this file and in the knowledge base.
