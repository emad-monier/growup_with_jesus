# Growing Up with Christ — Master Editorial Guidelines
**Version:** 1.0 (controlled standalone archival edition, 2026-10-17)  
**Provenance:** Reconstructed from the full editorial specification approved in this conversation. It preserves the substantive decisions and sections; this is not a claim of byte-for-byte export of a chat writing block.  
**Scope:** Boys' and girls' Coptic Orthodox growing-up guides, three languages each.  
**Status:** Working editorial standard; no ecclesiastical, clinical, copyright, or publication approval implied.

## 1. Project identity and vision
Two original, trustworthy, age-appropriate books serving Coptic Orthodox teenagers approximately 12–18, usable privately, with parents, or in Sunday School/youth meetings. They combine Orthodox spirituality, Scripture, verified Church Fathers, contemporary Coptic Orthodox pastoral sources, reliable medical and psychological evidence, and practical life skills. Secondary audiences include parents, servants, and mentors. Cultural scope: Egypt and global diaspora, especially Europe and English-speaking communities.

**Editorial motto (original, not an attributed quotation):** Teach the truth clearly. Explain it with love. Help the teenager understand how to live it. / علِّم الحق بوضوح، واشرحه بمحبة، وساعد الشاب على أن يعيشه. / Vermittle die Wahrheit verständlich. Erkläre sie mit Liebe. Hilf jungen Menschen, sie im Alltag zu leben.

## 2. Books and deliverables
- Boys: *Growing Up with Christ: A Coptic Orthodox Boy's Guide to Faith, Character, and Becoming a Man* — **B01–B23**, 23 working chapters; master target 50,000–65,000 words.
- Girls: *Growing Up with Christ: A Coptic Orthodox Girl's Guide to Faith, Confidence, and Becoming a Woman* — **G01–G26**, 26 working chapters; master target 55,000–70,000 words.
- Six full independent editions: boys/girls × EN/DE/AR. Typical chapter target 2,000–3,000 words, variable by subject. Output DOCX, print/digital PDF, EPUB after approval. Clarity and completeness outrank length.
- Working original outline chapter order is authoritative for the chapter registry; changes require a versioned crosswalk.

## 3. Pastoral writing standard
Inspired by general qualities associated with Pope Shenouda III: simple, biblically grounded, spiritually meaningful, practical, compassionate, reflective and hopeful. **Original prose only**: do not impersonate him, invent sayings, or closely reproduce protected text. Voice: a wise, caring, respectful mentor speaking directly to a teenager. Warm but not sentimental; honest without sensationalism; serious without fear or shame; confidence without arrogance. Examples before abstraction, plain terminology, no sermon-only chapters, no unsupported historical stories or fabricated statistics.

## 5. Orthodox theology and source policy
Present Trinitarian faith, creation in God's image, Incarnation, salvation, Holy Spirit, repentance, sacraments, life in the Church, communion of saints, and holiness through love, consistently with Coptic Orthodoxy. Source framework for **theological** claims: Bible; Coptic Orthodox liturgy/doctrine; verified writings of Pope Shenouda III, Bishop Moussa, Fr. Tadros Yacoub Malaty; identified patristic works; historical/educational materials. Scientific claims are governed separately by appropriate professional evidence.

Distinguish: **doctrine**, **pastoral guidance**, **historical tradition**, **cultural custom**, **modern editorial application**, and **empirical fact**. Do not infer Coptic universal doctrine from local Egyptian family customs. Account for Septuagint traditions, Psalm numbering, deuterocanonical books, and text differences. No fake Bible quotes.

## 6. Core Coptic source catalogue
- **Pope Shenouda III:** حياة الإيمان; حياة التوبة والنقاوة; الهدوء; حياة الرجاء; الوسائط الروحية; سنوات مع أسئلة الناس; المحبة قمة الفضائل; حياة التواضع والوداعة; خبرات في الحياة; الأسرة الروحية السعيدة. Spiritual voice, repentance, prayer, love, identity, family, practical wisdom.
- **Bishop Moussa (الأنبا موسى):** الشباب وحياة الطهارة; كيف أتخذ قرارًا؟; مكونات الشخصية الإنسانية; الشباب وتحديات المستقبل; كيف نتحدث إلى الشباب. Adolescent pastoral needs, responsible choices, chastity, communication.
- **Fr. Tadros Yacoub Malaty (القمص تادرس يعقوب ملطي):** دعوني أنمو! لمسات واقعية في عالم الشباب; الطهارة: أحاديث، أسئلة وإجابات; قاموس آباء الكنيسة وقديسيها. Adolescent development, body dignity, relationships and patristic context; historical medical claims need current review.
- **Church Fathers:** St. Athanasius; St. Cyril of Alexandria; St. Anthony; St. Macarius; St. Moses the Strong; St. Basil; St. John Chrysostom; St. Clement of Alexandria; sayings of Desert Fathers. Verify authenticity and provenance; beware unattributed popular maxims.
- **Liturgical and historical:** Agpeya الأجبية; Divine Liturgy القداس الإلهي; Synaxarium السنكسار; Psalmody الإبصلمودية; sacramental teaching; properly labelled hagiographical traditions.
- **Research libraries:** St-Takla.org, Coptic Treasures, and official Church/author archives where available. Hosting does not equal unrestricted republication rights.

## 7. Source and evidence verification
Before chapter drafting: define learning objectives; locate biblical references, Orthodox authored texts, authentic patristic passages, and current professional evidence; read relevant *sections*, not merely book titles; record exact author/work/edition/section/page when verifiable/URL/access date/permissions; flag interpretations and conflicts; produce an evidence brief. Source-topic mapping is **not** verified claim support. Do not invent pages, years, quotes, attributions or permission status.

For each claim store type, passage anchor, exact meaning, evidence status (verified/partial/unverified), reviewer, applicable chapter and language. Public notes should be unobtrusive but auditable; internal research logs must be detailed. Any direct quotation requires exact wording, context, identification and rights assessment. Translations of protected quotes may separately require rights.

## 8. Medical, psychological, developmental and scientific content
Use current relevant peer-reviewed evidence and recognized institutions (e.g. WHO, AAP, NHS/NICE, CDC, ACOG, German AWMF and German public-health/pediatric sources) for health and development. Review guideline publication/version dates. Religious writers are not automatically medical authorities, and medical guidance does not determine Orthodox theology. Teach normal puberty, menstrual variation, hygiene, anatomy, body image and when to seek help accurately, avoiding shame. For sexual subjects separate involuntary bodily feelings, behavior, Christian moral teaching and objective medical claims; avoid unsupported assertions about masturbation, pornography or development.

Discuss depression/anxiety, loneliness and eating disorders without stigma. Never blame insufficient faith for mental illness; spiritual support does not replace necessary clinical care. For substantial risk include country-appropriate help and emergencies.

## 9. Safeguarding and minors
Both editions teach autonomy, boundaries, consent, healthy relationships, grooming/abuse identification, reporting, and digital safety including cyberbullying, sextortion and AI deepfakes. The abuser is responsible for abuse; never silence a child for the reputation of family or Church, or frame forgiveness as remaining in danger. Spiritual authority never justifies exploitation; confession is not a substitute for mandatory safety/medical support. Age-level guidance and specialist safeguarding review required. Do not present ordinary menstruation as sin; distinguish practices surrounding Communion/fasting from verified doctrine, obtain pastoral review.

## 10. Gender equality and differentiation
Both editions affirm equal dignity, compassion, courage, education, self-control, service and leadership. Boys' chapters address male development, emotions, masculinity modeled by Christ, anger and respect without promoting dominance or suppression. Girls' chapters address female development, menstruation, body image, dignity, safety, leadership and independence without reducing worth to beauty, marriage or motherhood. Avoid double standards and generalizations.

## 11. Contemporary culture
Relevant to Egypt and diaspora, school, social and family environments, religious pluralism, peer influence, phones/social media/games/AI/deepfakes/online relationships, discrimination and social pressure. Apply ancient principles to modern issues **as modern interpretations**, not fictitious historic comments about technology.

## 12. Standard chapter template (adapt as appropriate)
1. Fictional, clearly presented relatable opening story (indicative 200–350 words).
2. Let's Talk About It — accessible topic explanation.
3. Understanding the Topic — evidence-based facts and age-appropriate context.
4. What Does Our Faith Teach? — Scripture and verified Coptic teaching.
5. Wisdom from Our Church — brief verified quote *or correctly attributed paraphrase*.
6. Questions Teenagers Ask — 3–6 respectful, direct Q&As.
7. Practical Guidance — safe feasible actions.
8. Think About It — 3–5 open reflections.
9. This Week's Challenge — optional safe action.
10. Prayer from the Heart — new original prayer, never falsely labelled ancient/liturgical.
11. References — traceable claims, short reader citations and detailed internal provenance.
Feature headings: Wisdom from Our Church / حكمة من كنيستنا / Weisheit aus unserer Kirche; Let's Talk Honestly / لنتحدث بصراحة / Reden wir offen darüber; What You Should Know / ما ينبغي أن تعرفه / Was du wissen solltest; Think About It / فكّر في الأمر / Denk darüber nach; This Week's Challenge / تحدّي هذا الأسبوع / Deine Herausforderung für diese Woche; Prayer from the Heart / صلاة من القلب / Ein Gebet von Herzen. Use selectively.

## 13. Visual and design standards
Unify covers, typography, chapter elements and Coptic aesthetics across the six editions, with culturally appropriate originals. Faith art respects traditional iconography; AI religious depictions must not be falsely labelled historic icons. Medical/anatomical visuals must be age-appropriate, factual, clinically reviewed. Arabic requires correct RTL design; accessibility and legally cleared image rights required.

## 14. Five review gates per chapter
1. Editorial: readability, structure, age appropriateness and usefulness.
2. Theological: Coptic doctrinal/pastoral accuracy, actual sources, doctrine vs custom.
3. Medical/psychological/safeguarding: qualified review where applicable.
4. Language: native-level EN/AR/DE wording and theological meaning parity.
5. Publication: copyright, permissions, references, design, print and technical checks.
A chapter is not 'approved for publication' until applicable gates are signed off for **each language edition**. AI may assist but is not ecclesiastical/medical authority.

## 15. Ethics, originality and rights
Books consist primarily of original prose, not copied excerpts. Verify Bible translation quotation limits and permissions (NKJV; Einheitsübersetzung 2016; Smith–Van Dyke), Coptic authors' rights, Church Fathers' particular modern translations, and artwork. Hosting online or no-fee access gives no automatic translation/republication license. Maintain permission register; never portray drafts as officially endorsed by the Church.

## 16. Production control and status
Maintain controlled files: EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES; BOYS/GIRLS manuscripts; THEOLOGICAL_REFERENCE_LIBRARY; CHAPTER_SOURCE_MAPPING; TRILINGUAL_GLOSSARY; FACT_CHECK_AND_REVIEW_LOG; COPYRIGHT_AND_PERMISSIONS_REGISTER. Every chapter stores immutable ID, version, date, writer/editor, evidence, review, translations and unresolved issues. Status: planned → sources identified → sources reviewed → draft → editorial revision → theological review → specialist review (where applicable) → translations → language review → approved for publication. Changes to theological meaning re-open all language checks.

## 17. Phased delivery

Research and drafting may overlap with evidence gates but publication reviews may not be skipped.

## 18. Twelve non-negotiable standards
1. No invented Scripture, theological/historical quotations, or medical claims.
2. No unverified sayings attributed to Church Fathers.
3. Never conflate Egyptian custom with binding Orthodox doctrine.
4. No fear/shame/humiliation as the main method of moral teaching.
5. Do not describe ordinary puberty or menstruation as sinful.
6. Do not substitute prayer for needed health, mental health, or safeguarding interventions.
7. Never minimize abuse or obstruct protection.
8. Do not treat religious authors as medical evidence without independent checking.
9. Preserve theology across translations.
10. Do not claim official Church approval without it.
11. Respect copyright and permissions.
12. Prioritize accuracy and reader welfare above output volume or speed.

## 19. Success definition and unresolved approvals
A chapter helps the reader understand, relate, learn Orthodox teaching, distinguish spiritual from scientific guidance, reflect safely, practice meaningful actions, and grow in faith and maturity. **Still pending**: qualified pastoral/theological approval; German Bible choice confirmation; quotation and reuse rights; medical/safeguarding review; full passage-level citations; native language proofreading and production approval.
