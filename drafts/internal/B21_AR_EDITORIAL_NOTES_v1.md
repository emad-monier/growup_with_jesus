## Claim and safety checks

- Opening scene is fictional; no real minor, family detail, or sensitive personal history is used.
- Leadership is consistently framed as service, not control; no universal Church rule about authority is stated.
- Service boundaries section names concrete triggers (secret from family, unwelcome touch, unsafe task, isolation) and routes them to a trusted adult; no diagnosis and no local safeguarding rule invented.
- Faith is never offered as a substitute for protection, a parent, a school counsellor, or a specialist.
- No shame language, no "earn your worth by serving" message, and no claim that tiredness cancels service.
- Local practices and emergency routes are left to responsible adults to adapt before publication.
- Ordinary dialogue in the story is deliberately **not** marked with `sacred-text` or `tradition-text`.
