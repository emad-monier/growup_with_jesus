# Phase 3C — Research and gate decision

## New evidence

- Hygiene and menstrual-product coverage now has accessible NHS-backed source candidates.
- Adolescent cycle variation checked against ACOG current-access patient education; 2015 Committee Opinion retained but explicitly flagged as older.

## Limits
- Claims are partially verified as research summaries; zero specialist-approved claims.
- Source texts are not bulk-copied, and quotation/translation permissions remain open.
- Pilot chapters may be drafted with review flags, not published.
- An independent clinical and ecclesiastical review is still mandatory.
