## Evidence

### [CLM:P3C001] pastoral
Fr. Tadros describes the human body as worthy of care and respect rather than contempt.
- أرع جسدك ولا تؤلهه! — 2 and 4; https://st-takla.org/books/fr-tadros-malaty/grow/care.html (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C002] pastoral
Fr. Tadros encourages adults to respond supportively to adolescents experiencing physical changes and new feelings.
- أرع جسدك ولا تؤلهه! — 2 and 4; https://st-takla.org/books/fr-tadros-malaty/grow/care.html (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C003] pastoral
Fr. Tadros rejects treating sexual development as inherently impure or shameful.
- الغريزة الجنسية في حياة المؤمن — opening and final paragraphs; https://st-takla.org/books/fr-tadros-malaty/grow/instinct.html (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C004] medical
Puberty involves changes such as underarm and pubic hair, genital growth and voice changes in boys, and breast development and menstruation in girls.
- Early or delayed puberty — Symptoms of early or delayed puberty; Non-urgent advice; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/early-or-delayed-puberty/ (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C005] medical
Puberty timing varies, and medical advice is appropriate if development appears unusually early or delayed.
- Early or delayed puberty — Symptoms of early or delayed puberty; Non-urgent advice; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/early-or-delayed-puberty/ (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C006] medical
Menstruation is a natural part of puberty; preparing young people to understand periods helps remove embarrassment.
- Starting your periods — Talking about periods; The questions girls ask about periods; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/periods/starting-periods/ (review: reviewed)

### [CLM:P3C011] safeguarding
Age-appropriate health education can cover puberty, menstruation, body image, privacy, consent, and seeking support.
- Comprehensive sexuality education — Key facts; What CSE covers; https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/comprehensive-sexuality-education (review: reviewed)
