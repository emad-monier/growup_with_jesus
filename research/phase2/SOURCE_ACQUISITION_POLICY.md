# Phase 2 acquisition and rights policy

- Current manifest entries are **source locations and manually inspected web sections**, not downloaded complete works.
- Do not assume web availability permits copying, translation, redistribution, embedding, or publication.
- Store precise URLs, source metadata and **original independent summaries** while permissions remain unknown.
- Save only minimal textual quotations after checking applicable legal allowances.
- Later ingestion of full texts needs case-by-case rights review and site access restrictions assessment.
- Each edition and version of a medical guideline must be tracked separately.
- Never classify St-Takla/Coptic Treasures site permissions as permission for our book series.
