# Editorial and expert review policy

**Source checked** means a referenced passage has been examined and found relevant. It does not mean expert-approved or publication-ready.

## Required reviewers

- **Coptic Orthodox clergy/theologian:** teaching, liturgy, pastoral questions, saint sources and biblical wording.
- **Medical specialist:** puberty and menstruation health facts, warning signs and current clinical relevance.
- **Safeguarding specialist:** bodily autonomy, abuse prevention, age suitability, reporting resources and trauma-sensitive language.
- **Language editor:** plain international English, Arabic الفصحى المبسطة and natural German using `du`.
- **Rights/editorial lead:** Bible editions, translations, protected quotations and reuse permissions.

## Stop conditions

Do not publish if any mandatory review is pending; if a citation refers to unsupported wording; if historical advice is represented as modern clinical evidence; if permissions are unresolved; or if an attributed Church-wide policy is not confirmed.

The question of menstruation and Holy Communion is **not resolved for universal/current practice** by the present source inventory. Attribution of a pastoral passage does not replace review.
