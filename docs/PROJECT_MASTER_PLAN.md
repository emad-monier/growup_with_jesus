# Growing Up with Christ — Project Master Plan
**Baseline:** Phase 0 / 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Consolidated, audited baseline; NOT publication-ready.

## Goal
Create two original, age-appropriate Coptic Orthodox growing-up guides (boys: 23 chapters; girls: 26), each in English, German, and Arabic (six complete editions), supported by verified theological, patristic and scientific sources.

## Agreed editorial standards
- Warm, simple, accessible pastoral prose inspired by general qualities of Pope Shenouda III, without impersonation or invented quotations.
- Arabic: الفصحى المبسطة; English: simple international English; German: einfaches natürliches Hochdeutsch (*du*, not formal *Leichte Sprache*).
- Bible: Smith–Van Dyke (Arabic), NKJV (English), provisionally Einheitsübersetzung 2016 (German).
- Prioritize Pope Shenouda III, Bishop Moussa, Fr. Tadros Yacoub Malaty, Coptic liturgy, and authenticated Fathers, with specialist medical/safeguarding resources for non-theological facts.
- Each chapter: story, explanation, faith teaching, verified Church wisdom, questions, practical guidance, reflection, challenge, prayer, references; adapt by topic.
- Do not claim endorsement by Coptic Orthodox hierarchy or publication readiness without human review.

## Roadmap and gates
0. **Consolidate and audit**: registry, decisions, manifest, integrity checks — THIS PACKAGE.
1. Design passage/claim/evidence and review schemas. **Gate:** traceable source and immutable IDs.
2. Acquire selected passages legally, checking URL and editions. **Gate:** precise anchors and permitted access.
3. Extract/verify granular claims and connect them to chapters. **Gate:** claim-to-evidence coverage.
4. Build retrieval-assisted writing and citation checks. **Gate:** reproducible chapter evidence brief.
5. Pilot puberty and menstruation chapters in three languages each. **Gate:** theological, clinical, safeguarding and language feedback.
6. Draft English and Arabic in batches. **Gate:** evidence-backed complete chapters.
7. Produce German adaptations. **Gate:** translation consistency and terminology review.
8. Qualified human editorial/professional review. **Gate:** issue closure and sign-off.
9. Design, permissions and publishing. **Gate:** approved six final editions.

## Execution constraints
Maintain chapter IDs B01–B23 / G01–G26; source IDs S01–S19 and M01–M16. Do not treat a source-topic link as verified support for claims. Keep rights metadata and exact evidence anchors. Review status must be explicit for each language edition.
